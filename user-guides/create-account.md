# How to Create an Account

## Purpose

This guide explains how new users can create an account on the application.
Follow the steps below to complete the registration process

## Requirements

- A smartphone.

- An active email address.

- An internet connection.

## Steps

1. Open the application.

2. Select **Sign Up**.

3. Enter your full name and email address.

4. Create a password.

5. Select **Create Account**.

6. Follow the instructions to verify your email address.

## Expected Result

Your account will be successfully created after verification.
